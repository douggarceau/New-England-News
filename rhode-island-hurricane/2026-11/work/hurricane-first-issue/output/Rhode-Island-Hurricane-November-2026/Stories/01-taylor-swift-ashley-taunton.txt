TAYLOR SWIFT’S $50,000 GIFT TO A HERO MOM

An I-95 rescue nearly killed Ashley Taunton. The teenager she helped was a friend’s son. Then a famous name appeared on the family’s fundraiser.

Before Taylor Swift’s name appeared on the fundraiser, Joe Taunton was counting a much smaller piece of good news: his wife could talk to him.

“At the end of the day, she’s communicating with me. And that’s the hope that I need right now,” he told NBC 10 on July 22.

Four days earlier, Ashley had been driving home from Newport with her daughter when a car went off southbound I-95 in Hopkinton during heavy rain. She pulled over, told her daughter to stay in the car and call her father, and went to help the teenagers who had crashed.

Then another vehicle hydroplaned toward them. According to the fundraiser organized for her family, Ashley pushed a teenager out of its path. The car struck her.

She was 42, a mother of three. She had left her own child in the car to help someone else’s.

Joe described injuries to her intestines and liver, a ruptured diaphragm that collapsed a lung, and severe damage to her leg. She was taken first to Westerly Hospital, then Rhode Island Hospital. By July 22, she had undergone three surgeries and come off life support.

The teenager was 19-year-old Vinny O’Rourke. His mother, Jamie, had been Ashley’s friend for 14 years. Ashley hadn’t recognized him at the crash scene. The connection emerged at the hospital when Jamie encountered Joe and the couple’s daughter. Her friend was the woman who had helped her son.

Ashley later told CT Insider that she couldn’t remember pushing Vinny clear. Other people had to tell her what she’d done.

In an August 17 post reported by CT Insider, she said she was back home in Ledyard, Connecticut, after about a month in the hospital.

Home still came with bills. The fundraiser set up by Laura Ortelt listed the costs that follow a crisis: groceries for the children, gas for hospital trips, household expenses and medical costs insurance wouldn’t cover. The family needed help keeping everyday life going while Ashley recovered.

Then came the donation: $50,000 from Taylor Swift. The gift was reported on August 28 and confirmed by NBC News.

“Wishing you the best recovery possible and sending love to your family!” Swift wrote in a message reported by Us Weekly.

Ashley told PEOPLE she was on her way to physical therapy when friends began calling Joe with the news. He had to step outside to collect himself. The family later received confirmation from GoFundMe that the donation really was from Swift.

There was a small Rhode Island connection already. Ashley told PEOPLE that her family works at a hotel on Misquamicut Beach, where they can see Swift’s house. Now the singer was helping with their bills while Joe cared for Ashley and the family.

In a September 1 interview with CT Insider, Ashley described therapy and medical appointments, and said a full recovery would likely take 12 to 24 months. She credited her Christian faith and the support of her community.

“I believe God has held me together until the doctors could put me back together,” she said.

By October 23, the family’s GoFundMe displayed $97,300 toward a $110,000 goal. Alongside the famous donor’s gift was the broader effort to help the Tauntons through the aftermath of a decision made in the rain on I-95.

Ashley had a less dramatic description of herself than the headlines did.

“I’m just a mom. Somebody was hurting. That’s just what you do,” she told PEOPLE.
