Belmont Dramatic Club Opens a Festival of New Short Plays, Nov. 14

Saturday, Nov. 14 at 8:00 PM at Belmont Town Hall in Belmont: nine original short plays by New England playwrights, running through Nov. 22.

Belmont Dramatic Club is presenting The Show Must Go On!, its first festival of short plays, at Belmont Town Hall, 455 Concord Avenue in Belmont. The festival includes nine original short plays by New England playwrights, staged by the club, a community theater group in Belmont.

Performance dates

Saturday, November 14 at 8:00 PM; Sunday, November 15 at 2:00 PM; Friday, November 20 and Saturday, November 21 at 8:00 PM; and Sunday, November 22 at 2:00 PM.

The festival runs across two weekends, with Friday and Saturday evening shows and Sunday afternoon performances, so the schedule suits both weeknight and weekend audiences.

What the plays explore

The plays look at how people perform in their daily lives and how they keep going when life is disrupted. Some pieces also give audiences a look backstage.

Festivals of short plays give a community theater company a chance to try many voices in one evening. Each piece tells its own story, so audiences can see a range of work without committing to a full-length production.

Tickets

Admission is $20. Tickets are available through belmontdramaticclub.org.
