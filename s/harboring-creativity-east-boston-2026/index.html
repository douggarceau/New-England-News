Harboring Creativity Brings More Than 20 Artists to ICA Watershed

Sunday, Nov. 8, from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at ICA Watershed, 256 Marginal St., East Boston.

The fourth annual Harboring Creativity exhibit and sale, hosted by the East Boston Artists Group, fills ICA Watershed with work by more than 20 local artists. Sunday's hours run from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

What's on view

Visitors will find original paintings, photography, mixed media, sculpture, jewelry and fiber art. The listing says the pieces are available for purchase, which gives visitors a way to take home work made by people in the neighborhood.

Meet the artists

A Meet the Artists reception was listed for Saturday, Nov. 7, from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. The listing describes the show as a way to browse original art, meet the artists and support East Boston's creative community.

For families

The listing describes the exhibit as family-friendly and lists it as suitable for groups, kids and rainy days. Admission is at no charge and open to all, so it works well as a small outing for a family or a group of friends.

Where to find it

The listing gives the Sunday hours as 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and places the show at ICA Watershed, 256 Marginal St. in East Boston, 02128. It is the fourth annual edition, according to the listing.
