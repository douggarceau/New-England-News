Geese Play Two Nights at Roadrunner, Nov. 10 and 11

The Brooklyn rock band takes over Boston's big Brighton hall for back-to-back shows, with Gwenifer Raymond opening.

Geese, one of the most talked-about young guitar bands around, plays two nights at Roadrunner in Brighton: Tuesday, Nov. 10, and Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Two straight nights at a room the size of Roadrunner says a lot about how quickly the band has grown. Their live sets lean loose and wiry, swinging from tight grooves to long, ragged jams, and they tend to sound a little different every night, which makes the double run a real temptation for fans.

Opening act

Guitarist Gwenifer Raymond opens both nights, so plan to be inside by the time doors open.

The details

Doors open at 7 p.m. and the show starts at 8 p.m. both nights, according to AXS. Roadrunner is at 89 Guest St. in Brighton. Tickets are on sale through AXS; prices were not posted on the listing we checked.
