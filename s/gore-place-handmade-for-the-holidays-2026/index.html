Handmade for the Holidays at Gore Place Brings 60 Makers to Waltham

Sunday, Nov. 8, from 10:00 a.m. at Gore Place, 52 Gore Street in Waltham, rain or shine.

Gore Place, the 19th-century estate at 52 Gore Street in Waltham, hosts its Handmade for the Holidays market on Sunday, Nov. 8. The listing describes 60 handmade vendors whom it calls local artisans, and it says the market runs rain or shine.

Shopping and the grounds

The market is a chance to shop early for holiday gifts, and visitors can also walk the grounds of the estate. The listing does not say which products the vendors sell. It says the market runs rain or shine, so visitors can plan on it going ahead in any weather. The event is a way to combine holiday shopping with a visit to a historic estate.

Animals and access

The listing notes that visitors can walk the grounds of the 19th-century estate and see heritage breed sheep on the property. Dogs are not permitted on the grounds, but service pets are welcome.

Hours

The listing gives 10:00 a.m. as the start time. It shows the end time as 2:00 p.m. in one field and 3:00 p.m. in the description, so check with the venue before you go.

Admission

The listing shows attendance at no charge, and the market is open to the public.
