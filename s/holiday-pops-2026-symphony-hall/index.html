Holiday Pops 2026: 41 Shows, Symphony Hall Dec. 3-28

The Boston Pops' holiday season adds a Muppet movie night and a sensory-friendly concert; tickets are on sale now.

The Boston Pops' Holiday Pops season runs Dec. 3 to 28 this year, with 41 concerts in all. Symphony Hall hosts both the first and last performances, and the orchestra also takes the show on the road around New England.

What's new this year

The Muppet Christmas Carol screens Dec. 27 and 28 with the Pops playing the score live, a natural fit for families. Seven kids' matinees, on Dec. 6, 12, 13, 19, 20, 23 and 24, feature the new BSO x NEC Honors Children Chorus. A new Bravo Club family morning on Dec. 19 at 9 a.m. pairs music with brunch and crafts, and a sensory-friendly concert on Dec. 5 lowers the volume and lighting and offers noise-reducing headphones and quiet rooms. Broadway star Sutton Foster headlines a fundraising concert on Dec. 9.

The traditional favorites are on the program too, including Sleigh Ride and The Twelve Days of Christmas, plus readings of 'Twas the Night Before Christmas, How the Grinch Stole Christmas! and A Christmas Carol.

Tickets

Boston tickets are on sale at bostonpops.org, by phone at 888-266-1200, or at the Symphony Hall box office, 301 Massachusetts Ave. Symphony Hall is next to the Symphony stop on the Green Line E branch. Prime December weekend dates go quickly, so book early.
