A Saturday Recital at Club Passim Showcases the School of Music

Saturday, November 14, with doors at 11 AM and the show at noon, at Club Passim in Harvard Square, Cambridge.

On Saturday, November 14, Club Passim opens its doors at 11 AM for a Passim School of Music recital, with the show starting at noon. Passim is one of the area's best-known listening rooms, and an early-afternoon recital is a rare chance to hear the school's work up close.

The listing tags the program with roots and traditional styles: Americana, bluegrass, Celtic, fiddle, old-time and singer-songwriter music. Performers and repertoire are not named on the page, which makes the afternoon a pleasant surprise for anyone who loves the sound of a roomful of strings.

About the school

Passim School of Music offers classes, workshops and one-on-one lessons with instructors, and the recital is a window into what happens in those rooms. For parents thinking about lessons, or adults who have always wanted to pick up an instrument, an hour in the audience is a fine way to hear what the school teaches.

Tickets and getting there

General admission is $15, and members are admitted at no charge. The listing also notes a $3 processing fee and a $2 preservation fee per ticket, with the processing fee waived at the box office. Club Passim sits in Harvard Square, a short walk from Harvard station on the Red Line.

Afternoon shows make it easy to pair a recital with lunch or a stroll around the Square before the music starts.
