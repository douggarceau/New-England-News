SoWa Christmas Market Opens Nov. 20 With 100-Plus Local Makers

Opening night is Friday, November 20, from 4:00 to 9:00 PM at SoWa Power Station in the South End.

Boston's SoWa Christmas Market returns for its 11th year. Opening night is Friday, November 20, from 4:00 to 9:00 PM at the SoWa Power Station on Harrison Avenue in the South End, and the market runs through December 27.

The indoor market brings together more than 100 local makers, artists, designers and specialty food vendors. The organizers list categories including apparel and accessories, bath and body, candles, children's goods, home decor, jewelry, stationery, prints and art, toys and games, and gourmet gifts, as well as pet products.

Hours change through the season. In November the market is closed on Mondays, open Tuesday through Thursday from 4:00 to 8:00 PM, open Friday from 4:00 to 9:00 PM, open Saturday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM and open Sunday from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. It is closed on Thanksgiving, and on Black Friday, November 27, it is open from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

From December 1 the evening hours run later on Fridays and Saturdays, and the market opens on Christmas Eve from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. It is closed on Christmas Day. Because hours can shift for special days, check the organizer's site before you visit.

A market gathered in one place makes it easy to shop for the holidays while talking with the people who made what is on the tables. It is a good first stop for anyone who wants gifts with a local story.
