Sing We Noel: Christmas Music From England and Early America

Sunday, December 6, at 6:30 PM at Gordon Chapel in Old South Church, Back Bay.

On Sunday, December 6, at 6:30 PM, Camerata presents Sing We Noel, a program of Christmas music from England and early America. The concert takes place in Gordon Chapel at Old South Church, on Boylston Street in Back Bay, a short walk from Copley Square.

The program's title and description point to a repertoire of carols and sacred song from two traditions that shaped how the season sounded in both places. Early music in a church setting tends to be intimate, so the evening suits listeners who want to hear voices and small ensembles up close rather than in a large hall.

Tickets range from $11 to $81. The listing links to the ticket seller from its event page, and it does not state an RSVP or registration requirement, so it is worth checking the ticket page before you go. The chapel is at 645 Boylston Street, Boston, MA 02116.

For anyone planning a December outing, this is a quieter alternative to the larger holiday productions downtown. Arrive a few minutes before the 6:30 PM start to find your seat and take in the room.
