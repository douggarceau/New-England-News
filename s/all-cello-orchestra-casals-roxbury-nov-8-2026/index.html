48 Cellos Honor Pablo Casals in Roxbury, Nov. 8

Sunday, Nov. 8 at 3 p.m. at the Roxbury Community College Media Arts Center, marking Casals's 150th birthday, with no charge admission.

Forty-eight cellists will fill the stage at Roxbury Community College on Sunday afternoon, Nov. 8, to celebrate the 150th birthday of Pablo Casals. The Catalan cellist is remembered by the presenters as a cellist and humanitarian, and this concert gives his music a full, rich sound.

The ensemble

The All-Cello Orchestra brings together students and faculty from Berklee, Boston Conservatory at Berklee, Boston University, Longy School of Music and New England Conservatory. Eugene Friesen conducts. Vivo Performing Arts presents the concert, with thanks to Lizbeth and George Krupp for their leadership of its Roxbury programming.

The program

The program moves from tango to Brazil and back to Catalonia. It includes Carlos Gardel's Por Una Cabeza, Gabriel Faure's Pie Jesu from the Requiem, and Villa-Lobos's Bachianas Brasileiras No. 5, with the soprano Nicole DiPasquale. Several works are by Casals himself, among them Nigra Sum, El cant dels ocells (Bird Song), Sant Marti del Canigo and a Sardana for cello orchestra. A newer piece by Mike Block, When the Birds Sing Along in the Morning, draws on Casals's memoir Joys and Sorrows.

Admission and details

Admission is no charge, and the event page recommends the concert for ages 4 and up. The page links to a parking and accessibility page for the Media Arts Center, rather than printing a street address, so check that page before you go. Reminders can be requested from the event page.

The page describes the cello's range as one that overlaps with the human voice, which is part of what makes forty-eight of them sound so moving together. It is a rare chance to hear a whole orchestra of cellos, and a fine afternoon for music lovers of every age.
