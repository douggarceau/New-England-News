Journey's Final Frontier Tour Rolls Into TD Garden Nov. 10

Neal Schon, Jonathan Cain and Arnel Pineda bring an evening with Journey to Boston on a Tuesday night.

Journey brings its Final Frontier Tour to TD Garden on Tuesday, Nov. 10, billed as an evening with the band, meaning no opener and a long set from the arena-rock giants.

The fall leg added 40 more North American dates to the tour, and Boston is one of them. For a band whose catalog doubles as the soundtrack of a few generations of road trips and ballgames, a full evening means plenty of room for the deep cuts as well as the singalongs.

Who's on stage

Founding guitarist Neal Schon leads the lineup with longtime keyboardist and guitarist Jonathan Cain and lead singer Arnel Pineda. Deen Castronovo is on drums and vocals, with Jason Derlatka on keyboards and vocals and Todd Jensen on bass and vocals. Drummers in the house will want to keep an eye on Castronovo, who has long pulled double duty behind the kit and on the mic.

Getting there

TD Garden sits on top of North Station, served by the Orange and Green lines and Commuter Rail. Parking can be reserved ahead through Ticketmaster. VIP ticket holders should check tour emails for door times and entry locations. Suites can be booked through TD Garden at 617-624-1847.
