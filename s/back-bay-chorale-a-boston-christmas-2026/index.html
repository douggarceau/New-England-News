Back Bay Chorale's Christmas Concert at Old South Church, Dec. 18-19

Friday, Dec. 18 at 7:30 PM and Saturday, Dec. 19 at 4:00 PM at Old South Church, 645 Boylston Street, Boston.

The Back Bay Chorale's holiday program, A Boston Christmas: Creative Gifts, comes to Old South Church on Boylston Street for two performances in mid-December. The first is on Friday, December 18, at 7:30 PM. The second is on Saturday, December 19, at 4:00 PM.

The program draws on carols from King's College, Cambridge, and on other choral works from the English tradition. Stephen Spinelli conducts the chorale, and the concert listing includes organ, brass and percussion.

The evening's centerpiece is the world premiere of the 2026 Back Bay Chorale Carol Commission. A newly written piece sits inside a program of familiar music, so listeners who know the carols will hear one that has not been sung before.

Tickets and admission

General tickets are sold through the chorale's ticket links, one for each night. The chorale's page does not list a general admission price. Guests who hold an EBT card are offered $5 admission, and the chorale asks them to leave a name and phone number at 617-648-3885 to reserve.

Old South Church is at 645 Boylston Street in Boston. Check the chorale's page for any updates before you go.
