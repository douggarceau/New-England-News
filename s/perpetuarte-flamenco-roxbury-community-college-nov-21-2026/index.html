Flamenco Duo Triana Maciel and Nino de los Reyes Plays Roxbury, Nov. 21

Saturday, Nov. 21, at 3:00 PM at the Roxbury Community College Media Arts Center, 1234 Columbus Avenue in Roxbury Crossing, with no charge admission.

Vivo Performing Arts presents the flamenco duo Triana Maciel and Nino de los Reyes in PerpetuARTE, a program at the Roxbury Community College Media Arts Center on Saturday, Nov. 21. The show starts at 3:00 PM and the listing runs until 5:00 PM.

The listing describes PerpetuARTE as an all-new program about the connective power of art, with themes of creativity, humanity, authenticity, passion and interdependence. It is a small-scale chance to see two artists in a neighborhood venue, the kind of afternoon that rarely gets much attention in the press.

Admission

Admission is at no charge. The listing does not mention a reservation requirement.

Getting there

The Media Arts Center is at 1234 Columbus Avenue in Roxbury Crossing. The presenting organization, Vivo Performing Arts, runs performances across the city and in neighborhood venues like this one.
