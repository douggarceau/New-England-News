Rosin Brings Its 'Disreputable Classical Music' to Club Passim

Friday, Nov. 13 at 8 p.m., with doors at 7 p.m., at Club Passim in Cambridge.

Rosin plays Club Passim in Cambridge on Friday, November 13. Doors open at 7 p.m., and the music begins at 8 p.m. The listing shows $35 general admission and $33 for members, with a small processing fee and preservation fee added to each ticket.

Disreputable Classical Music

The group calls its sound 'disreputable classical music,' a phrase that suits a band that does not let genre labels decide what it plays. Its repertoire draws on classical, contemporary and fiddle music. The group says it wants to bring instrumental music into livelier rooms, and it describes its playing as if the music could be taken away after the show.

A Room With a History

Club Passim has been a listening room for decades. Its early years as Club 47 drew folk performers including Joan Baez and Bob Dylan, and the room has since welcomed songwriters and acoustic musicians from many traditions. A show there is an intimate way to hear a band up close.

Tickets

Tickets are available through the Tickets link on the club's event page. The club's directions and parking page explains how to reach the room. The group's own website is rosin.band.
