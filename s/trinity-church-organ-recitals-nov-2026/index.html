Friday Organ Recitals at Trinity Church in Copley Square

Fridays at 12:15 p.m. on Nov. 6, 13 and 20 at Trinity Church, 206 Clarendon St., Back Bay.

Trinity Church in Copley Square is hosting a series of midday organ recitals this November. Each one is held on a Friday at 12:15 p.m., which makes it an easy break from a busy workday or a relaxed stop on a fall afternoon.

The recitals fall on November 6, 13 and 20. Admission is no charge, and the church suggests a $10 donation toward the music program. The series offers a quiet half hour of organ music inside one of the city's great interiors.

Trinity Church was designed by the architect H. H. Richardson and completed in the 1870s, and it is among the most celebrated buildings in Boston. Its stained glass and carved woodwork make the sanctuary an ideal setting for organ music, and few places in the city pair architecture and sound this well.

Plan Your Visit

The church is at 206 Clarendon Street, on the Copley Square side of Back Bay, and is an easy walk from Copley station on the Green Line. Arrive a few minutes early to find a seat. The schedule and recital details are posted at https://trinitychurchboston.org/music/organ-recitals/
