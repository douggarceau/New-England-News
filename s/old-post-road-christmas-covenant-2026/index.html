Old Post Road Musicians' Christmas Program at Church of the Covenant

Saturday, Dec. 5 from 4:00 to 5:30 PM at 67 Newbury Street, Back Bay, with Convivium Musicum.

Musicians of the Old Post Road will present Christmas in Olde/New England on Saturday, December 5, from 4:00 to 5:30 PM at the Church of the Covenant, 67 Newbury Street in Back Bay. The concert is presented with the early music choir Convivium Musicum.

The program gathers Christmas choral and instrumental music from churches on both sides of the Atlantic. Among the works are early American fuguing tunes and pieces in the West Gallery style, the lively parish-church singing of 18th-century England that also took root in New England worship. Music by early American composers rounds out the evening.

The listing notes that the program marks the 250th anniversary of American independence.

Tickets

Admission is listed at $10 to $55. The listing does not mention a reservation requirement, so check the ticket source before you go.
