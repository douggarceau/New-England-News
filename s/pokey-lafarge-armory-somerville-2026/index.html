Pokey LaFarge Celebrates Rent Money at the Armory in Somerville

Tuesday, November 10 at 7 p.m. (doors 6:30 p.m.) at the Center for the Arts at the Armory, 191 Highland Avenue in Somerville.

Pokey LaFarge performs at the Center for the Arts at the Armory in Somerville on Tuesday, November 10, in a show celebrating the release of Rent Money, his new album. Doors open at 6:30 p.m., and the music starts at 7 p.m. in the Performance Hall at 191 Highland Avenue.

LaFarge is an Illinois-born musician who has been recording and performing for about twenty years. His music moves easily across many styles of twentieth-century American song, and the presenter describes Rent Money as the best set of songs he has made so far.

Before you go

Tickets are sold through AXS, and the listing does not show a price, so check the ticket page for the current figure. Food and drinks are served at the Performance Hall bar when doors open. The Armory is a community arts center, and a weeknight show in its hall is an easy way to see a working American roots musician up close, in a room far smaller than an arena.
