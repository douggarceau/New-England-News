Convivium's A North Atlantic Christmas Comes to All Saints in Brookline

Saturday, Nov. 21, from 7:00 to 8:30 PM at All Saints Parish, 1773 Beacon Street in Brookline, with tickets at $25.

On Saturday evening, Nov. 21, the ensemble Convivium presents A North Atlantic Christmas at All Saints Parish in Brookline. The concert runs from 7:00 to 8:30 PM at 1773 Beacon Street and is built around Christmas and devotional music from both sides of the Atlantic.

The program

The first part of the program brings English Christmas pieces by Tallis and Byrd. It then turns to villancicos and motets from Portugal, Spain and Mexico City, including Victoria's O Magnum Mysterium. The program also includes works by early New England composers Billings and Ingalls.

A little history

William Billings, a Boston composer of the late 1700s, published hymn collections that helped shape choral singing in New England. Putting his work beside the older European repertoire shows how the same sacred texts were set in very different places.

Tickets

Admission is $25. The listing does not describe how to buy tickets or register, so check convivium.org for details before you go.

Convivium is the ensemble behind the concert, and its listing points to convivium.org for more about the group and its season. This is a concert for listeners who like their holiday music a little older than the usual sleigh bells, and who enjoy hearing carols and motets sung in their original languages.
