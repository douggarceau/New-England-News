Poiesis Quartet Plays Beethoven and Prokofiev at the Gardner, Nov. 8

Sunday, Nov. 8, from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m. in Calderwood Hall at the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, 25 Evans Way in the Fenway.

The Poiesis Quartet performs in Calderwood Hall at the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum on Sunday, Nov. 8, from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m. The concert is part of the museum's Fall 2026 Weekend Concert Series, which runs on select Sundays from Sept. 13 through Nov. 22.

The program

The listing names four works: Beethoven's final string quartet, Prokofiev's String Quartet No. 1, Sky Macklay's Many Many Cadences, and Tapestry of the Beloved Beatified by Jeff Scott, which the listing describes as a new commission. The mix runs from Beethoven's last quartet to a new commission, a span of about two centuries.

The ensemble

The quartet was founded in 2022. According to the event listing, The New York Times named it among the best classical performances of 2025, and the group has quickly drawn attention in classical music.

The hall and the series

Calderwood Hall is a 300-seat room, and the listing notes that about 80 percent of its seats are front row. The Weekend Concert Series dates to 1927 and, according to the listing, is the longest-running museum music program in the country. The concert fills most of a Sunday afternoon, and the listing gives the address as 25 Evans Way, Boston, MA 02115.

Tickets and details

Tickets are listed at $70 to $99. Seniors pay $63 to $89, and students and children ages 5 to 17 pay $25. The museum's listing for the concert is at gardnermuseum.org/calendar/poiesis-quartet-20261108. Visitors who want the full set of details can also find them on the Boston Calendar page for the concert, which repeats the start and end times.
