Doja Cat Brings 'Tour Ma Vie' to TD Garden on Nov. 23 With Latto

The Grammy winner's world tour hits Causeway Street the Monday before Thanksgiving, with Latto as special guest.

Doja Cat's Tour Ma Vie World Tour arrives at TD Garden on Monday, Nov. 23, the biggest pop arena date on Boston's November calendar. Rapper Latto joins the bill as special guest.

It is one of the hottest arena tickets of the fall, and the show is staged for a full house.

The details

Doors open at 6:30 p.m. and the show starts at 7:30 p.m. TD Garden is at 100 Legends Way, directly above North Station, so the Orange and Green lines and Commuter Rail all drop you at the door.

Tickets and entry

Tickets are on sale now through Ticketmaster, linked from the TD Garden event page. The venue has not posted prices. Garden parking can be bought ahead through Ticketmaster, which is worth doing on a weeknight show.

General admission floor ticket holders come in through the regular doors and pick up wristbands at the floor entrance. The Garden says there is no early lineup or early access for GA fans before doors open, so there is no advantage to camping out on the sidewalk. VIP ticket holders should check the tour's emails for their own door times and entry points.

Before you go

A Monday night arena show on Thanksgiving week means heavy traffic around North Station. Taking the T and arriving close to doors is the easy play. Groups of 18 or more can rent a suite through TD Garden.
