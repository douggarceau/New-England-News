Doja Cat Brings 'Tour Ma Vie' to TD Garden on Nov. 23 With Latto

The Grammy winner's world tour hits Causeway Street the Monday before Thanksgiving, with Latto as special guest.

Doja Cat's Tour Ma Vie World Tour arrives at TD Garden on Monday, Nov. 23. Rapper Latto joins the bill as special guest.

The details

Doors open at 6:30 PM and the show starts at 7:30 PM. TD Garden is at 100 Legends Way in Boston.

Ticket information is linked from the TD Garden event page. Check your ticket for entry details before you go.
