Piano Stations Open at Boston Public Library Branches This November

Walk-in piano stations from Nov. 1 through Nov. 30 at Grove Hall, Hyde Park, Lower Mills and Teen Central.

The Boston Public Library is offering piano learning stations at several of its locations this month, through a program that the library says makes it the first U.S. public library system to take part in the Lang Lang Foundation's Keys of Inspiration initiative. From November 1 through November 30, visitors can walk in and try the pianos, subject to availability.

The stations are at the Grove Hall, Hyde Park and Lower Mills branches, along with Teen Central at the Central Library in Copley Square. The program runs on a walk-in basis, so there is no appointment to book and no need to plan far ahead.

A library setting puts the piano within reach of neighborhood families and young people who might never have sat down at a keyboard. For a first try, a public branch on a weekday is a low-pressure way to find out whether you like playing.

Visit

Check each branch's hours on the library's website before you go, since availability depends on the day.
