Boston Tuba Christmas Brings Brass to The Steps Plaza, Nov. 28

Saturday, November 28, from 12:30 to 1:30 PM at The Steps Plaza in Downtown Boston, with a festive tuba and brass program.

Boston Tuba Christmas takes over The Steps Plaza in Downtown Boston on Saturday, November 28, 2026, from 12:30 to 1:30 PM, the Saturday after Thanksgiving. It is a festive, brass-filled holiday gathering that is easy to walk up to on a lunch break or a family outing.

The event page says the gathering began in New York City in 1974 and has been held at The Steps Plaza since 2021. It calls the Boston edition a festive extravaganza.
