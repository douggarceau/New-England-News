Masterworks Chorale Sings Mozart's Coronation Mass, Nov. 22

Sunday, Nov. 22 from 3:00 to 5:00 PM at Cary Memorial Hall in Lexington: Masterworks Chorale, Kevin Leong and the Pro Arte Chamber Orchestra.

Masterworks Chorale opens a Sunday afternoon of Mozart and the Haydns on November 22, 2026, from 3:00 to 5:00 PM at Cary Memorial Hall, 1605 Massachusetts Avenue in Lexington. Music Director Kevin Leong leads the chorale, which is joined by soloists Deborah Selig, soprano; Roselin Osser, mezzo-soprano; Gregory Zavracky, tenor; and Will Prapestis, baritone, and by the Pro Arte Chamber Orchestra.

The program

Mozart's Coronation Mass is the featured work. The program also includes Mozart's Dixit et Magnificat, Joseph Haydn's Responsoria, and Michael Haydn's Timete Dominum and Beatus vir, so the afternoon sets the two composers' sacred music side by side.

About the chorale

Mozart completed the Coronation Mass in 1779 while working in Salzburg, and the afternoon pairs it with other sacred works from the same circle of composers, so audiences can hear how the music of the period was built for the church.

The group was founded in 1940 as the Lexington Choral Society and renamed Masterworks Chorale in 1969 to reflect its geographically diverse membership. Allen Lannom conducted the chorus from 1952 to 2005, and Steven Karidoyanes became Music Director in 2006.

The Pro Arte Chamber Orchestra accompanies the choral works, so the sound is full-scale rather than a single keyboard. The concert runs about two hours, from 3:00 to 5:00 PM.
