Jan Lisiecki Plays Mendelssohn at Symphony Hall, Nov. 19-21

Pianist Jan Lisiecki joins conductor Samy Rachid and the BSO Nov. 19, 20 and 21 at Symphony Hall on Massachusetts Avenue.

After a week of oratorio and world premiere, the Boston Symphony Orchestra turns to the lyrical heart of the Romantic repertoire. Pianist Jan Lisiecki plays Mendelssohn's Piano Concerto No. 1, framed by Weber and Schumann, in a program led by conductor Samy Rachid.

The program is part of the BSO's 2026-27 season under Music Director Andris Nelsons, which runs from Sept. 18 to May 9. It arrives between two weeks of the orchestra's Creation festival and a late-November program with pianist Jean-Yves Thibaudet, making it the quieter, more traditional stop in a busy month at Symphony Hall.

The program

The evening opens with the overture to Weber's Oberon. Lisiecki then takes the solo part in Mendelssohn's Piano Concerto No. 1. The concert ends with Schumann's Symphony No. 1, nicknamed Spring.

The artists

Marc Mandel's Friday talk is a good way to get oriented before the Schumann, and the Friday afternoon time suits listeners who prefer a daytime concert. Thursday and Saturday are evening performances, so there is a choice of schedules whether you are visiting from out of town or live nearby.

Jan Lisiecki is the soloist, and Samy Rachid conducts the Boston Symphony Orchestra.

Dates and times

Performances at Symphony Hall are Thursday, Nov. 19 at 7:30 p.m.; Friday, Nov. 20 at 1:30 p.m., preceded by a talk by Marc Mandel from 12:15 to 12:45 p.m.; and Saturday, Nov. 21 at 8 p.m.
