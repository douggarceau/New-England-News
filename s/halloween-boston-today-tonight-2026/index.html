Halloween in Boston: Today and Tonight

Coco with a live orchestra at Symphony Hall, glowing pumpkins at the Stone Zoo, then a 1920s horror classic at Symphony Hall and Halloween shows from Allston to Cambridge.

Boston's Halloween starts with the kids and ends with a silent-film scare. Here's the day in order, from an afternoon movie concert to the late shows.

For kids and families

At 1:30, Symphony Hall screens Disney and Pixar's Coco with the score played live, a perfect Día de los Muertos-season outing for the whole family. After dark, the Stone Zoo in Stoneham lights its Jack-O'-Lantern Nights trail.

Tonight, for grown-ups

The night's most Boston pick is back at Symphony Hall at 9: The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari, the 1920 German horror classic, screened with live music. Jazz fans have pianist Benny Green at Scullers, Club Passim has Peter Mulvey, and the jam band moe. plays the Wilbur. Cambridge and Somerville fill out the night with costume-friendly cover and tribute shows.
