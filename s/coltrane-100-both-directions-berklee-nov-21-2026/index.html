Coltrane 100 Tribute Brings Five Players to Berklee, Nov. 21

Saturday, Nov. 21, at 8:00 PM at the Berklee Performance Center in Back Bay, with Joe Lovano, Melissa Aldana, Leo Genovese, Linda May Han Oh and Jeff 'Tain' Watts.

Coltrane 100: Both Directions at Once marks the 2026 centennial of John Coltrane's birth. Five players gather on one stage to reflect on his influence across generations, geographies and musical traditions.

The players

Joe Lovano and Melissa Aldana play tenor saxophone. Leo Genovese is on piano, Linda May Han Oh plays bass and Jeff 'Tain' Watts is on drums. Together they present the concert as a collective tribute rather than a single-artist show.

The concert

The performance begins at 8:00 PM on Saturday, Nov. 21, at the Berklee Performance Center. The presenter, Vivo Performing Arts, describes the evening as a reflection on how Coltrane's music has traveled across generations and cultures.
