Commonwealth Shakespeare's A Christmas Carol at the Majestic, Dec. 11-20

Dec. 11 to 20 at the Emerson Cutler Majestic Theatre, with the first preview at 7:00 PM on Friday, Dec. 11.

The Commonwealth Shakespeare Company is bringing its holiday production of Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol to the Emerson Cutler Majestic Theatre for a run from Dec. 11 through Dec. 20, 2026. Steve Wargo wrote the adaptation, Dianne Adams McDowell arranged the music, Steven Maler directs, and Dan Rodriguez leads the musical direction. The production uses 19th-century carols to tell the familiar story of Ebenezer Scrooge.

The first preview is Friday, Dec. 11, at 7:00 PM, followed by a preview on Saturday, Dec. 12, at 2:00 PM. The press opening is Saturday, Dec. 12, at 7:00 PM. Regular performances then run on Sunday, Dec. 13, at 2:00 PM; Wednesday, Dec. 16, at 7:00 PM; Thursday, Dec. 17, at 7:00 PM; and Friday, Dec. 18, at 7:00 PM. The final weekend includes Saturday, Dec. 19, at 2:00 PM and 7:00 PM, and Sunday, Dec. 20, at 2:00 PM.

Several performances are built for wider access. The Saturday, Dec. 19, 2:00 PM show and the Sunday, Dec. 20, 2:00 PM show include audio description with a tactile tour that meets in the lobby one hour before curtain. The Saturday, Dec. 19, 7:00 PM show is ASL-interpreted, and the Sunday, Dec. 20, 2:00 PM show also has ASL interpretation. Every performance is open captioned.

The show runs about two hours with one 15-minute intermission. Tickets are sold through the Buy Tickets link on the company's page. The Emerson Paramount Center Box Office, at 559 Washington Street, is open Thursday through Saturday from 12:00 to 6:00 PM and can be reached at 617-824-8400 or tickets@emersontheatres.org. Groups of 10 or more get 10 percent off through groupbooking@bochcenter.org or 617-532-1116. In-person student rush tickets are $25 and go on sale two hours before each performance while supplies last.

Arrive early for the box office and allow time to find the lobby before any tactile tour. This production gives families a classic story with live music to start the holiday season.
