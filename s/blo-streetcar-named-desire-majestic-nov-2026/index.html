Boston Lyric Opera Opens 'A Streetcar Named Desire' This Week

Thursday, Nov. 5 and Friday, Nov. 6 at 7:30 p.m. and Sunday, Nov. 8 at 3 p.m. at the Emerson Cutler Majestic Theatre on Tremont Street.

Boston Lyric Opera opens André Previn's A Streetcar Named Desire at the Emerson Cutler Majestic Theatre this week, with performances on Thursday, November 5 and Friday, November 6 at 7:30 p.m. and a matinee on Sunday, November 8 at 3 p.m. The opera sets a libretto by Philip Littell to Tennessee Williams' Pulitzer Prize-winning play. This production is a new co-production with Opera Theatre of Saint Louis and is sung in English with English supertitles.

A Summer in New Orleans

The story unfolds during a sweltering New Orleans summer. Blanche DuBois moves in with her sister Stella and brother-in-law Stanley, and her romantic view of the past meets his blunt realism. Previn's score gives that meeting a lyrical voice, which makes the opera a fine introduction for anyone who knows Williams' play and for anyone who has never seen it.

The Company

Daniela Candillari conducts, and Patricia Racette directs. Brittany Renee sings Blanche, Angela Yam sings Stella, Thomas Glass sings Stanley and Brenton Ryan sings Mitch.

Performances and Tickets

The Emerson Cutler Majestic Theatre is at 219 Tremont Street in Boston. Tickets are sold through Boston Lyric Opera's online ticketing page, and the company also offers a general ticketing link for the rest of its season. Arrive a few minutes early to find your seat before the performance begins.
