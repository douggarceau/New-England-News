Laurie MacAllister and Seth Glier Sing at Club Passim, Nov. 21

Saturday, Nov. 21, with doors at 7:00 PM and the show at 8:00 PM at Club Passim in Cambridge.

Singer-songwriters Laurie MacAllister and Seth Glier share a bill at Club Passim on Saturday, Nov. 21. Doors open at 7:00 PM and the show starts at 8:00 PM.

Laurie MacAllister

MacAllister co-founded the Americana trio Red Molly, which released seven albums. She launched a solo career in late 2024 with a show at Club Passim. Her 2018 solo album, The Lies the Poets Tell, pays tribute to the contemporary folk scene.

Seth Glier

Glier is a Grammy-nominated singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist. His sixth album, The Coronation, was released on MPress Records. He has won five Independent Music Awards and has performed with James Taylor and Ani DiFranco.

Tickets

The listing gives the price as $30 general admission or $28 for members. All fees are shown as included, with a $3 processing fee and a $2 preservation fee that is waived at the box office. The listing does not give a street address, so check the Club Passim page before you go.
