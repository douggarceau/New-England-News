Copley Square Lights Its Tree and Reopens the Park, Nov. 30

Monday, Nov. 30, from 5:00 to 6:00 PM at 560 Boylston Street, with arts and crafts, Santa and Rudolph, and music from the Boston Children's Chorus.

Boston's Parks and Recreation Department will light the Copley Square tree on Monday, November 30, from 5:00 to 6:00 PM, in the plaza at 560 Boylston Street. The city's listing says the evening celebrates the grand reopening of Copley Square Park along with the annual tree lighting.

What happens

The program includes arts and crafts, appearances by Santa Claus and Rudolph, and musical performances by vocalist Julia Chisholm, the Boston Children's Chorus and Rey Royale. Afterward, cookies and pictures with Santa are offered at the Fairmont Copley Plaza starting at 6:00 PM.

Getting in

No registration is listed, and the city lists the event at no charge. Friends of Copley Square, BXP and the Fairmont Copley Plaza are named as partners, with sponsorship from H.P. Hood LLC and additional support from Drake's Cakes. Questions can go to the Parks Department at 617-635-4505 or parks@boston.gov.
