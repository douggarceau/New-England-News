Boston Today and Tonight: Creation, Tierney Sutton and Passim

Tonight at 7:30 PM, Osvaldo Golijov's Creation premieres at Symphony Hall in Back Bay, with jazz at Regattabar and folk at Club Passim at 7:00 PM.

Thursday's biggest draw is the world premiere of Osvaldo Golijov's Creation, a Boston Symphony Orchestra commission. Smaller rooms in Cambridge and Back Bay offer jazz and folk on the same evening, and Berklee Performance Center's family musical Emperor's New Clothes begins at 7:00 PM for younger audiences.
