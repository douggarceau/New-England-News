Second Saturdays: Volunteers Care for Jamaica Pond on Nov. 14

Saturday, Nov. 14 from 10 a.m. to noon at Jamaica Pond, with the Emerald Necklace Conservancy's monthly volunteer stewardship morning.

On Saturday, November 14, from 10 a.m. to noon, volunteers can join staff of the Emerald Necklace Conservancy for a morning of land stewardship at Jamaica Pond. The conservancy looks after the historic parks designed by Frederick Law Olmsted, and this month's work centers on Jamaica Pond.

A Monthly Tradition

Second Saturdays is a monthly volunteer program, and the work moves to a different park in the Emerald Necklace each month. The same series also meets in the Back Bay Fens, so volunteers who want to keep showing up can follow the schedule from one park to the next.

What to Expect

The listing does not spell out specific tasks, so dress for outdoor work and expect a practical morning among neighbors who care about the parks. Registration is required. After signing up, participants receive an email with waivers to sign, meeting details and what to expect.

Meeting Spot and Questions

The meeting place is the James Michael Curley House on Jamaicaway. The listing gives the address as 350R Jamaicaway, Boston, and asks volunteers to confirm the exact spot after registering. For questions, email volunteer@emeraldnecklace.org or call (617) 522-2700.
