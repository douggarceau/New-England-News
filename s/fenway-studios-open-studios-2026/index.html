Fenway Studios Opens Its Doors to More Than 25 Artists This Weekend

Through Sunday, Nov. 8, at 5:00 p.m., at Fenway Studios, 30 Ipswich Street, in the 1905 National Historic Landmark building.

Fenway Studios' Open Studios Weekend Celebration runs from 11:00 a.m. Saturday, Nov. 7, to 5:00 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 8. Visitors can meet more than 25 artists, view their work and tour the studios in the 1905 building, which is a National Historic Landmark.

A look inside

Open studios let visitors see where the work is made as well as the finished pieces, and the listing says they can see the work of more than 25 artists in one weekend. The listing describes the weekend as a chance to meet local artists in their own spaces, and it calls it an annual event.

A food drive

The 18th annual food drive benefits The Home For Little Wanderers and runs alongside the open studios, so visitors can bring donations when they visit. The listing asks visitors to bring Thanksgiving-style donations, including stuffing, gravy, cranberry sauce, canned yams, peas or carrots, cake and pudding mixes, chocolate candies and cookies.

Admission

The 1905 building is listed as a National Historic Landmark, so the weekend also offers a look at a historic building as well as the artists. The listing shows admission at no charge, so the weekend is open to anyone who wants to walk through the building and meet the artists.
