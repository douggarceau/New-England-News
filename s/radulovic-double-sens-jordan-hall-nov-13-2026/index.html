Nemanja Radulović and Double Sens Make Their U.S. Debut, Nov. 13

Friday, Nov. 13, at 8:00 PM at NEC's Jordan Hall, 30 Gainsborough Street, in the Fenway neighborhood of Boston.

Serbian-born violinist Nemanja Radulović, who makes his home in Paris, brings his Double Sens Chamber Orchestra to Boston for a U.S. debut on Friday, Nov. 13. The concert, presented by Vivo Performing Arts, starts at 8:00 PM in NEC's Jordan Hall, and the listing sets the evening from 8:00 to 10:00 PM.

The listing describes the evening as moving between classical masterworks and folk traditions. It names no individual works, and it notes that the program was changed for an earlier performance, so check the Vivo performance page for the current repertoire before the concert.

A soloist and an ensemble on one stage

Radulović performs as the soloist with the Double Sens ensemble, a format in which the violin line and the orchestra share the stage. Chamber orchestras of this kind are a natural setting for a violinist who moves between concert repertoire and folk material, because the smaller forces let the solo voice sit clearly inside the texture.

Jordan Hall, the NEC concert hall at 30 Gainsborough Street, is built for chamber music. Its acoustics suit string ensembles, and audiences who come to hear a soloist in a room this size tend to hear every shift in dynamics. For a U.S. debut, the hall offers a close, attentive setting.

Tickets and details

Single tickets are sold through Vivo Performing Arts, which lists multiple price points. An Applause Circle membership, $25, gives early access to tickets, with details at vivoarts.org/applause-circle. The concert starts at 8:00 PM on Friday, Nov. 13. Confirm the current program on the Vivo performance page before you go.
