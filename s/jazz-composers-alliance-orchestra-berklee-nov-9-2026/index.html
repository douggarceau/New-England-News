Jazz Composers Alliance Orchestra Plays Four Berklee Faculty Composers

Monday, Nov. 9 at 8 p.m. at the Berklee Performance Center, with music by four Berklee faculty composers.

The Jazz Composers Alliance Orchestra plays the Berklee Performance Center on Monday, November 9, at 8 p.m. The program presents music by four Berklee faculty composers: Bob Pilkington, Dave Harris, Mimi Rabson and Darrell Katz.

A Familiar Address

The orchestra has played this hall before. The group's own website lists a November 2025 concert at the Berklee Performance Center on Massachusetts Avenue, so this evening continues a relationship between the ensemble and the college's main stage.

The Music

The listing does not name individual pieces, so the evening is best understood as a showcase of how four composers who teach at Berklee write for a large jazz ensemble. Listeners who enjoy big-band writing, with composed sections and improvised solos, will find this a natural fit.

Tickets

Tickets are sold through Etix. Check the Etix link for current prices. The Berklee box office is at 136 Massachusetts Avenue and can be reached at 617-747-2261 or boxoffice@berklee.edu to confirm hours.
