Halloween Night in Boston: What to Expect

A Saturday Halloween, a sunny weekend in the forecast, costume shows all over town, and the clocks fall back overnight.

Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, so families get a full night out and the clubs get a full weekend. Here's what to know.

The weather

Hoodline's weekend outlook calls for a sunny Halloween weekend in Boston after a stretch of warm days. Evenings cool off fast this time of year, so plan a layer under the costume and check the forecast before you head out.

Trick-or-treat

Boston doesn't set one citywide trick-or-treat time; most neighborhoods go out from late afternoon into the evening. The sun sets before 6 p.m., so most of the night happens in the dark. Glow sticks, a flashlight or reflective tape make kids easier to see.

Don't forget the clocks

Daylight saving time ends at 2 a.m. Sunday, Nov. 1. Clocks fall back an hour, so the late shows get a bonus hour.
