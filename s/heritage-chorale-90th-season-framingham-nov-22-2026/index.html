Heritage Chorale's 90th Anniversary Season Opens in Framingham, Nov. 22

Sunday, Nov. 22, from 4:00 to 6:00 PM at Nevins Hall, 150 Concord Street in Framingham, with four soloists and an 18-piece orchestra.

Heritage Chorale opens its 90th anniversary season on Sunday, Nov. 22, with a program of Handel and Haydn at Nevins Hall in Framingham. The listing runs from 4:00 to 6:00 PM, and the concert is billed as the Chorale's 90th anniversary concert.

The listing describes four outstanding soloists, an eighteen-piece orchestra, and a collaborative pianist and music director for the Chorale. It does not name the individual soloists or the works on the program, so check the Chorale's website for those details.

Tickets and location

Nevins Hall is at 150 Concord Street in Framingham. Admission is $25. Tickets are sold through the Chorale's online box office at heritagechorale.org, which the listing says is now open. No reservation requirement is stated.

A milestone season

A choral concert that marks an anniversary gives audiences a chance to hear a long-running community chorus in full voice. This one pairs that milestone with the music of Handel and Haydn, two composers whose works fill concert programs across the choral world.
