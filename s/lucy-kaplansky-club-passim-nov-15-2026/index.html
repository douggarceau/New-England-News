Lucy Kaplansky Sings at Club Passim, Nov. 15

Sunday, November 15, with doors at 6:00 PM and the show at 7:00 PM at Club Passim in Cambridge.

Lucy Kaplansky performs at Club Passim on Sunday, November 15, with doors at 6:00 PM and the show at 7:00 PM. The Passim listing describes her as an acclaimed singer-songwriter of rare talent.

Tickets are $40 general admission and $38 for members, sold through the Tickets link on the Passim page. The listing notes a processing fee and a preservation fee per ticket, and the processing fee is waived at the box office.

Passim lists the afternoon set by Jenna Moynihan and Owen Marshall on the same day, so a folk fan could make a full day of it, with the early show at 2:00 PM and this one at 7:00 PM.

Arrive a little before the 6:00 PM doors if you want a good table in the room, and plan the ride home with the evening's start time in mind.
