Oriana Consort Sings Tennyson, Folk Songs and Poulenc, Nov. 22

Sunday, Nov. 22 at 5:00 PM at First Lutheran Church on Berkeley Street in Back Bay: the Oriana Consort's fall concert.

The Oriana Consort, a chorale of about 25 adult mixed voices directed by Walter Chapin, gives its fall concert at First Lutheran Church, 299 Berkeley Street, on Sunday, November 22, at 5:00 PM. The listing runs to about 7:00 PM.

The program

Two settings of Tennyson's The Lotos-Eaters, by Robert Hobby and Daniel Gawthorp, open each half of the concert. Between them come British Isles folk songs by Gawthorp, Ralph Vaughan Williams, Alice Parker and Ēriks Ešenvalds, French songs by Debussy, Saint-Saëns and Poulenc, and sacred works by Salamone Rossi, Randall Thompson, Charles Villiers Stanford, Williametta Spencer, Ešenvalds and Rosephanye Powell.

Tennyson's poem describes sailors who settle into a languid island life, and the concert opens with two settings of it. The program then moves from English poetry to folk song, French art song and sacred music, a range that suits a chorale of about 25 voices.

About the group

Founded in 1994 and based in Cambridge, the Oriana Consort aims for what it describes as a warm, attractive and compelling choral sound.

The listing gives an end time of about 7:00 PM, so the concert fits an early Sunday evening. The church sits in Back Bay near Copley Square.

Tickets

Tickets are $25 or $20, and buying online in advance saves $5 per ticket. Order at orianaconsort.org/upcoming-concerts.
