Hannibal Buress Brings His Stand-Up to the Wilbur on Nov. 20

One of comedy's driest, most offbeat voices plays Boston's home of stand-up on a Friday night.

Comedian Hannibal Buress plays the Wilbur Theatre on Friday, Nov. 20, bringing his deadpan, sideways style to the Theater District's best-known comedy room.

Buress is known for a loose, deadpan act that wanders off on tangents, the kind of set that rewards longtime fans and wins over newcomers.

The details

Doors open at 6:30 p.m., and the show starts at 7:30 p.m. The Wilbur Theatre is at 246 Tremont St., across from the Boston Common side of the Theater District, a short walk from Boylston on the Green Line and Tufts Medical Center on the Orange Line. Ticket prices were not posted on the listing we checked; tickets are sold through the Wilbur.

Make it a night

The Wilbur sits in the middle of the Theater District, with Chinatown restaurants a couple of blocks away for dinner before the show. A Friday stand-up show is one of the easier date nights in the city to plan: arrive at doors, grab your seat, and let someone else do the talking for 90 minutes.
