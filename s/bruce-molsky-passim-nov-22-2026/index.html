Bruce Molsky Plays Old-Time Fiddle at Club Passim, Nov. 22

Sunday, Nov. 22, with doors at 1:00 PM and the show at 2:00 PM at Club Passim in Cambridge.

Bruce Molsky, a Grammy-nominated old-time and traditional musician, brings his fiddle to Club Passim in Cambridge on Sunday afternoon. He also plays banjo, guitar and sings.

The music

The Club Passim listing describes his concerts as intimate and warm. His repertoire mixes Southern Appalachian tunes with traditional material from around the world, so the afternoon moves easily between regional styles.

About the artist

Molsky is known mainly for his fiddle playing. The listing also notes his role as a Visiting Scholar at Berklee College of Music and his many collaborations and recordings, which give a sense of how long he has worked in the tradition.

Times and place

Doors open at 1:00 PM and the show starts at 2:00 PM on Sunday, Nov. 22. The listing does not give a street address for the venue, so check the Club Passim page for directions and parking before you go.

Tickets

The listing gives the price as $35, or $33 for members. All fees are shown as included, with a separate $3 processing fee and $2 preservation fee that is waived at the box office.
