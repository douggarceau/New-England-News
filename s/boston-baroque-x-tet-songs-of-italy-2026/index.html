Boston Baroque's X-tet Sings Songs of Italy on Nov. 13 and 14

Friday and Saturday, November 13 and 14, at Seully Hall in the Boston Conservatory at Berklee, with soprano Amanda Forsythe.

Boston Baroque's string ensemble, the X-tet, returns this month with a program its own announcement describes as a sweeping celebration of Italian baroque and Renaissance music. The concerts take place on Friday, November 13 and Saturday, November 14, in Seully Hall at the Boston Conservatory at Berklee.

The evening opens a window onto the Italian sound that shaped European music for generations. Works by Vivaldi and Albinoni sit beside pieces by Castello, Handel, Maria Teresa Agnesi and Antonio Archilei, whose song "Dalle più alte sfere" is one of the highlights.

What to listen for

Handel's cantata Armida abbandonata puts the spotlight on the soprano Amanda Forsythe, a GRAMMY-winning singer who joins the ensemble for the concerts. The viola da gamba player Cristiano Contadin brings the warm, grounded tone of that instrument to the repertoire, which suits the intimate scale of the X-tet.

A folk-inspired Tarantella Gargano adds a dance-like lift to the middle of the program. The evening closes with Morgantina Studies, a new work by the composer Colin Jacobsen, which carries the program from centuries-old Italian sources into a contemporary voice.

Dates, venue and tickets

The two concerts are on Friday, Nov. 13 and Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026, at Seully Hall, Boston Conservatory at Berklee. The season page we checked does not list start times or a street address for the hall, so check the listing before you go. It gives no ticket prices either. It does say that subscriptions and memberships bring savings, so the ensemble's own site is the place to start.

If the X-tet concerts whet your appetite, Boston Baroque's season continues with Handel's Messiah on Dec. 12 and 13 at Jordan Hall, a classic way to spend a December evening with baroque music.
