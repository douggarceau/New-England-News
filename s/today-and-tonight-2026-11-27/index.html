Boston Today and Tonight: Messiah, Gershwin and Chris Botti

Handel's Messiah at 7:30 PM and Chris Botti at 8:00 PM headline a Friday night, with a Gershwin matinee at 1:30 PM.

Friday after Thanksgiving is a busy night for music in Boston. Symphony Hall hosts a matinee of Gershwin and Respighi and the first evening of Handel and Haydn's Messiah, while the Wilbur Theatre features trumpeter Chris Botti. Club Passim in Cambridge adds a folk show at 7:00 PM for those who want something smaller.
