Berklee Singers Night Takes On the Music of Journey, Nov. 17

Tuesday, Nov. 17 at 8 p.m. at the Berklee Performance Center, 136 Massachusetts Avenue in the Back Bay.

Each semester, the Berklee Voice Department turns one of its most prestigious showcases over to a single songbook. This semester the songbook belongs to Journey, and on Tuesday, Nov. 17, the stage at the Berklee Performance Center will be filled with student voices working through the band's best-known catalog.

Auditions and the showcase

The event page says that 250 talented Berklee vocal students auditioned for a place in Singers Night. It does not say how many will perform, but it calls the evening one of the Voice Department's most prestigious showcases. A program PDF is linked from the event page for those who want the running order in advance.

Tickets and the box office

Tickets are sold through the etix link on the Berklee page, and prices are not listed there. Box office hours are noon to 6 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, plus two hours before ticketed Berklee Performance Center events. Hours can change, so the page suggests emailing boxoffice@berklee.edu or calling 617-747-2261 before you visit.

Why it is worth an evening

Student showcases like this one are where many young singers first test their nerve in front of a full house. An evening of Journey, sung by Berklee's rising voices, is a warm and lively way to hear the next generation of performers, and a good reason to be in the Back Bay on a Tuesday night.
