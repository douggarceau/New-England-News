Boston Today and Tonight: Monét, Creation and Charlap

Friday, Nov. 13: Victoria Monét at MGM Music Hall at 8:00 PM, Golijov's Creation at Symphony Hall at 7:30 PM and the Bill Charlap Trio at Regattabar at 7:00 PM.

Friday, November 13 offers a full evening of concert halls across Boston and Cambridge. Victoria Monét brings her Frequency of Love tour to MGM Music Hall at Fenway, the Boston Symphony Orchestra performs Osvaldo Golijov's Creation at Symphony Hall, and the Bill Charlap Trio plays Regattabar in Cambridge.
