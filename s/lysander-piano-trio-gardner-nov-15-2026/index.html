Lysander Piano Trio Plays Grieg and Dvořák at the Gardner, Nov. 15

Sunday, Nov. 15, from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m. in Calderwood Hall at the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, 25 Evans Way.

The Lysander Piano Trio plays Calderwood Hall on Sunday, Nov. 15, from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m. as part of the Gardner's Fall 2026 Weekend Concert Series, which the listing describes as a ten-concert season.

The program

The program includes works by Amanda Maier, Edvard Grieg and Antonín Dvořák. It also features Bongani Ndodana-Breen's Two Nguni Dances, which draws on sub-Saharan traditions, including those of the composer's Xhosa heritage.

The listing says the program centers on Maier, a Swedish violinist-composer who worked with Brahms and Grieg. The Gardner is at 25 Evans Way in Boston, 02115, and the afternoon concert runs from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m.

The ensemble

According to the listing, the trio met as students at Juilliard. The Washington Post praised the group for "an uncommon degree of heart-on-the-sleeve emotional frankness."

The hall

Calderwood Hall is a 300-seat room, and most of its seats are in the front row, per the listing. The Weekend Concert Series dates to 1927, and the listing calls it the longest-running museum music program in the country.

Tickets

Tickets are listed at $70 to $99, with seniors at $63 to $89 and students and children ages 5 to 17 at $25.
