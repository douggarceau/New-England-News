The 48th Boston International Antiquarian Book Fair Opens at the Hynes

Friday, November 6 through Sunday, November 8 at the Hynes Convention Center in Back Bay, with about 110 rare book dealers.

The 48th Boston International Antiquarian Book Fair opens on Friday, November 6, at the Hynes Convention Center in Back Bay and runs through Sunday, November 8. About 110 rare book dealers are bringing first editions, manuscripts, maps and autographs, and they travel to Boston from the Czech Republic, Denmark, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Serbia, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and 24 states.

Hours are 4 to 8 p.m. on Friday, noon to 7 p.m. on Saturday and 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Sunday. A Friday ticket costs $25 and is good for all three days, Saturday is $15 and Sunday is $10. Students and children 12 and under are admitted at no charge, and librarians and staff of nonprofit cultural institutions can register in advance with an organizational email address.

For new collectors

The fair is a good place to start. The dealers have set aside "Discovery" items priced at $100 or less for people who are just beginning to collect, so a visit can be as much a lesson as a shopping trip. A walk along the aisles puts handwritten pages and early printed books in front of you that are rarely seen outside a museum.

Check the fair's own pages before you go for the current dealer list and any schedule changes.
