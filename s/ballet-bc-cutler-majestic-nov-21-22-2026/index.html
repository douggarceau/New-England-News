Ballet BC Brings Contemporary Dance to the Cutler Majestic, Nov. 21-22

Saturday, Nov. 21, at 8:00 PM and a Sunday afternoon show, Nov. 22, at the Cutler Majestic Theatre on the Emerson College campus in Boston.

Ballet BC, the Vancouver company led by artistic director Medhi Walerski, comes to Boston this month for two performances at the Cutler Majestic Theatre at Emerson College. The Saturday show, Nov. 21, begins at 8:00 PM. A Sunday afternoon performance, Nov. 22, also runs; confirm its start time on the Vivo Performing Arts listing before you go.

Presented by Vivo Performing Arts, the program is described as an evening about tenderness, ecstasy and human longing, danced by a company of 20 dancers. The listing does not name the individual works or their choreographers, so the program itself is the surprise. What it does promise is a contemporary idiom in which movement carries the story.

A Vancouver company on a Boston stage

This visit brings the company's work to a Boston audience that has a long tradition of ballet, with the Boston Ballet's seasons at the Boston Opera House and the Nutcracker that fills the calendar each winter. Ballet BC offers a different experience. Its contemporary repertory is built around dancers who move with weight, speed and emotional directness, and a single evening lets a viewer see a full company rather than a few guest soloists.

The listing's focus on tenderness and longing points to a program that is as much about feeling as technique. For a program built on physical expression, the scale of the house matters. The Cutler Majestic is a smaller theatre than the big civic halls, so the dancers are close enough that the emotional arc of the evening should read clearly from most seats.

Where to see it and how to get tickets

The Cutler Majestic Theatre is at 219 Tremont Street, on the Emerson College campus. Single tickets are sold through Vivo Performing Arts, which lists multiple price points. An Applause Circle membership, $25, gives early access to tickets, with details at vivoarts.org/applause-circle. Plan for the Saturday show at 8:00 PM or the Sunday afternoon performance, and check the Vivo listing for the Sunday start time before you buy.
