Boston Ballet's 'Nutcracker' Returns to the Opera House Nov. 27

Mikko Nissinen's holiday staple opens the day after Thanksgiving and runs through New Year's Eve.

Boston's biggest holiday tradition on the ballet stage is almost back. Boston Ballet's production of The Nutcracker opens at the Citizens Opera House on Friday, Nov. 27, the day after Thanksgiving, and runs through Thursday, Dec. 31.

Choreographed by artistic director Mikko Nissinen, the production sets Tchaikovsky's score, played live by the Boston Ballet Orchestra, against a wintry storybook world of snowflakes, sweets and a nutcracker prince. For many local families it is the official start of the season, and the opening-weekend matinees tend to go first.

The details

The run is the only Boston Ballet program on the calendar in November and December, part of the company's 63rd season. The Citizens Opera House is at 539 Washington St. in Downtown Crossing, steps from the Orange and Red lines. Opening day includes a 1:30 p.m. performance; the full performance schedule and prices are on Boston Ballet's site.

Plan ahead

The Opera House is coming off the fall run of Wicked, which closes Nov. 15, so the turnaround is fast. Tickets for weekend matinees in December and for the Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve performances are usually the toughest to find, so families should book soon.

Looking further ahead

After the holidays, the company's season continues with its Winter Experience program, Feb. 18 to 28, followed by Coppelia in March and Cinderella from late April into May.
