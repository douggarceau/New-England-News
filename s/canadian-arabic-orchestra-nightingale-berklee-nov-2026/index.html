Nightingale: Canadian Arabic Orchestra Honors Abdel Halim Hafez

Saturday, Nov. 14 at 7:30 p.m. at the Berklee Performance Center on Massachusetts Avenue, with songs by Abdel Halim Hafez.

The Canadian Arabic Orchestra presents Nightingale at the Berklee Performance Center on Saturday, November 14, at 7:30 p.m. The concert, presented by Whoome Productions, celebrates the romance and nostalgia of Arabic music's golden age through songs by Abdel Halim Hafez.

The Artists

Mohammad El Mougy, grandson of the Egyptian composer Mohamed El Mougy, is among the featured performers. He appears alongside Maple Arts Group and musicians from the Canadian Arabic Orchestra. The evening is built around the Hafez repertoire, which has a loyal following among listeners of Arabic song.

The Venue

The Berklee Performance Center is at 136 Massachusetts Avenue in Boston. Its box office is open from noon to 6 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, plus two hours before ticketed events.

Tickets

Tickets are sold through Etix. The Berklee box office can confirm hours, which may change during breaks and holidays. Call 617-747-2261 or email boxoffice@berklee.edu before you visit.
