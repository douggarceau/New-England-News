Jenna Moynihan and Owen Marshall Play Club Passim, Nov. 15

Sunday, November 15, with doors at 1:00 PM and the show at 2:00 PM at Club Passim in Cambridge.

On Sunday, November 15, Jenna Moynihan and Owen Marshall play Club Passim in Cambridge. Doors open at 1:00 PM and the show starts at 2:00 PM, which makes this an easy afternoon outing for anyone who loves Celtic and traditional music.

Moynihan is a freestyle fiddler whose playing draws mainly on Scottish tradition, with influences from American, Irish and Swedish music. She has toured with several acts, has performed as a soloist with the Boston Pops, and teaches internationally. She is an assistant professor of strings at Berklee College of Music.

Marshall is a multi-instrumentalist who plays guitar, mandolin and banjo, and he is known for his bouzouki playing and melodic guitar work. He has toured and recorded with groups including the Seamus Egan Project and Dervish, and he has long accompanied traditional musicians.

Tickets are $35 general admission and $33 for members, sold through the ticket link on the Passim event page. The listing does not state a separate reservation requirement.

Because the set begins early in the afternoon, it also pairs well with the evening show by Lucy Kaplansky, which Passim lists the same day.
