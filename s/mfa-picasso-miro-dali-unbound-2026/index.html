Picasso, Miró and Dalí Turn the Page at the MFA Through Jan. 24

Through January 24, 2027, the MFA's Torf Gallery shows Spanish artists' illustrated books, with a virtual lecture on December 9 at 6:30 PM.

Picasso, Miró, Dalí: Unbound is on view at the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston through January 24, 2027, in the Lois B. and Michael K. Torf Gallery (Gallery 184). The exhibition gathers works by three Spanish artists in the form of livres d'artiste, illustrated books in which painters and writers shape images and words together on the page.

The museum says many of the works are rarely on view. Featured pieces include Salvador Dalí's 1974 illustrations for Sigmund Freud's Moses and Monotheism, Juan Gris and Pierre Reverdy's Au Soleil du Plafond, and Joan Miró's Le Lézard aux plumes d'or (The Lizard with Golden Feathers), a book with 40 color lithographs.

Plan your visit

The MFA is at 465 Huntington Avenue in Boston. Tickets are sold through the museum's website at mfa.org/tickets, and admission and hours are listed at mfa.org/visit. A related program is a virtual member lecture also called Picasso, Miró, Dalí, on Wednesday, December 9, 2026, from 6:30 to 7:30 PM.
