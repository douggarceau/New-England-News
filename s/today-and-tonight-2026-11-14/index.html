Boston Today and Tonight: Creation, Nightingale and Arsenal Bazaar

Saturday, Nov. 14: Snowport opens at 10:00 AM, Nightingale plays Berklee at 7:30 PM and Creation plays Symphony Hall at 8:00 PM.

Saturday offers a holiday market by the harbor, a Watertown bazaar, a library recital and a full evening of concerts. Snowport in the Seaport is open from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and the Arsenal Holiday Bazaar in Watertown runs from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
