A Family Musical Retells 'The Emperor's New Clothes' at Berklee

Thursday, Nov. 12 at 7 p.m. at the Berklee Performance Center, a musical for families based on Hans Christian Andersen's classic tale.

The Berklee Theater Arts Collaborative and its Theater for Young Audiences Ensemble present a musical version of The Emperor's New Clothes at the Berklee Performance Center on Thursday, November 12, at 7 p.m.

A Classic Tale, Set to Music

The production retells Hans Christian Andersen's story of a vain ruler and two swindlers who promise him a suit that only the honest can see. The adaptation was written by René Pfister, artistic director of the Berklee Theater Arts Collaborative, and it is made for family audiences.

A Night Out for Young Audiences

Shows made for young audiences bring families into a theater together, and this one suits a Thursday evening for children and the grown-ups who come with them. Its songs and its familiar story give younger viewers a way into the theater.

Tickets

Tickets are sold through the Berklee Performance Center's Etix page. The box office at 136 Massachusetts Avenue is open from noon to 6 p.m. Tuesday through Friday and two hours before ticketed events.
