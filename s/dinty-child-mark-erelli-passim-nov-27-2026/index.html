Dinty Child and Mark Erelli Share Songs at Club Passim, Nov. 27

Friday, Nov. 27, with doors at 6:00 PM and the show at 7:00 PM at Club Passim in Cambridge.

Dinty Child and Mark Erelli bring a collaborative show to Club Passim on Friday, Nov. 27. The evening features live versions of the two artists' co-written songs.

An up-close evening

The listing calls the night an intimate, up-close look at the partnership between the two musicians. It describes Erelli's singer-songwriter style blending with Child's inventive musicianship, so the songs are heard in the voices of both writers.

Times

Doors open at 6:00 PM and the show begins at 7:00 PM. The Club Passim listing does not state a street address, so check the venue's directions and parking page before you go.

Tickets

The listing gives the price as $40 general admission or $35 for members. All fees are shown as included, with a $3 processing fee and a $2 preservation fee that is waived at the box office.
