Places We Carry: SMFA at Tufts Juried Student Show Opens at the MFA

Opens Saturday, Nov. 21, in the Edward H. Linde Gallery at the Museum of Fine Arts in Fenway and runs through April 18, 2027.

The Museum of Fine Arts is opening a show that puts emerging artists at the center. Places We Carry, a juried student exhibition from the School of the Museum of Fine Arts at Tufts, opens Saturday, Nov. 21, in the Edward H. Linde Gallery, also known as Gallery 168, at the museum at 465 Huntington Avenue in Fenway. It runs through April 18, 2027.

The show is part of the museum's ongoing relationship with the school and was organized with support from Tufts University Art Galleries. The exhibition page lists five artists: Liangsi Wang, Bianca Daher, Co&Lee, Antonio Angel and Christine Chiang.

What the artists explore

The work centers on migration and identity. Several of the artists draw on their own experiences or on the journeys of their relatives, and the exhibition presents those personal stories as its starting point. The page does not give the number of works in the show, so plan to take your time in the gallery.

Planning your visit

Opening day is Saturday, Nov. 21, and the show stays on view through April 18, 2027. The exhibition page lists no reception time and no RSVP, and it does not say whether a timed ticket is needed for the gallery, so check the museum's ticketing and hours pages before you go. The gallery sits inside the museum at 465 Huntington Avenue, in Fenway.
