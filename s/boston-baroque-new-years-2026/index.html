Boston Baroque Rings In the New Year at Sanders Theatre

Thursday, Dec. 31 at 7:30 PM and Friday, Jan. 1 at 3:00 PM at Sanders Theatre in Cambridge, with Beethoven's Seventh after intermission.

Boston Baroque is ringing in 2027 with its New Year's Celebration Concert at Sanders Theatre, the historic hall inside Harvard Yard at 45 Quincy Street in Cambridge. The main performance is on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, at 7:30 PM. A second performance follows on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027, at 3:00 PM, which gives an afternoon option for anyone who would rather start the new year with a concert.

The program is built around Mozart and Beethoven. Filippo Ciabatti conducts the evening, which opens with the overture to Mozart's Figaro and continues with Mozart's Horn Concerto, K. 412, with Todd Williams as soloist. Beethoven's Creatures of Prometheus overture comes next, followed by his Romance No. 2 for violin, with Christina Day Martinson as soloist. The concert closes after intermission with Beethoven's Symphony No. 7.

Plan for about two and a half hours with one 20-minute intermission. The company's page also links a press release about a conductor and program change, so check the page for the latest lineup before you leave the house.

Tickets are priced from $25 to $125 and are sold through the Buy Tickets link on the company's page. Subscribers receive 10 percent off. The box office can be reached at 617-987-8600 on weekdays from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, or by writing to boxoffice@bostonbaroque.org.

Sanders Theatre is at 45 Quincy Street, Cambridge, MA 02138. Arrive early enough to find your seat before the 7:30 PM start on New Year's Eve, and allow extra time for the walk through the Yard if you are coming from Harvard Square.
