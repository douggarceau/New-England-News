Huntington Premieres 'Adia & Clora Snatch Joy' Nov. 24 to Dec. 13

Opens Tuesday, Nov. 24 and runs through Dec. 13 at the Huntington Theatre on Huntington Avenue, with a press opening on Dec. 2.

The Huntington Theatre Company opens the world premiere of Adia & Clora Snatch Joy on November 24, with performances through December 13. The play is the final chapter of Mfoniso Udofia's nine-play Ufot Family Cycle, and it is set in the South Carolina marshes.

Two Daughters and a Search for Joy

Clora Abernathy and Adiaha Ufot are two devoted daughters who begin to explore desire, pleasure and joy for themselves. Udofia wrote the book, libretto and lyrics. Michael Ellis Ingram composed the score, which is sung a cappella, so the harmonies of the company's voices carry much of the storytelling. Awoye Timpo directs.

Performance Dates

The press opening is Wednesday, December 2 at 7 p.m. An open-captioned performance takes place Tuesday, December 8 at 7 p.m., and an audio-described performance is set for Saturday, December 12 at 2 p.m. Those performances suit audiences who want captioned or audio-described access.

Getting There and Tickets

The Huntington Theatre is at 264 Huntington Avenue in Boston. Tickets are available through the Huntington's production page, where the full performance schedule appears in the dates and times picker.
