Arlington Open Studios Brings 80 Local Artists to Town Hall and Maple Street

Saturday, November 7 from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. at Arlington Town Hall, the Arlington Community Center and Robbins Library in Arlington.

Arlington Center for the Arts presents the 28th annual Arlington Open Studios on Saturday, November 7, from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., a town-wide showcase of work by more than 80 local artists. Painters, photographers, fiber artists, ceramicists, glass artists, jewelers, printmakers and sculptors all take part, and most visitors can buy work directly and meet the people who made it.

The day spreads across three connected stops. Arlington Town Hall, at 730 Massachusetts Avenue, has more than 40 artists on two floors, and the Arlington High School Band plays on the Town Hall steps from noon to 4 p.m. The Arlington Community Center and the Arlington Center for the Arts, at 27 Maple Street, hold a second group of more than 40 artists on the ground and first floors, plus a ceramics demonstration from noon to 4 p.m. in the ACA Clay Studio. Robbins Library, at 700 Massachusetts Avenue, adds live readings from the Red Letter Poets.

Galleries, puppets and food

Two exhibitions give the day a second layer. The Shaira Ali Gallery shows "Opposites Attract," and the Hallway Gallery shows "Inside ACA." Puppet performances by Margaret Moody and friends are scheduled for younger visitors, and The Roasted Granola sells food at the Maple Street entrance. Admission is no charge.

A town-wide open studio day is one of the best ways to meet the artists behind the work you see on gallery walls. The day falls on a single Saturday, so it is worth mapping out which buildings to see before you set out.
