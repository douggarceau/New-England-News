Troy Roberts Quartet Plays Arrow Street Arts Tonight, Nov. 9

Monday, Nov. 9, at The Studio at Arrow Street Arts, 2 Arrow Street in Cambridge, with doors at 6:30 PM and music at 7:30 PM.

Troy Roberts, a GRAMMY-nominated saxophonist based in New York City, brings his quartet to Cambridge tonight for a jazz evening at The Studio at Arrow Street Arts. Roberts, an Australian immigrant, is a prolific bandleader with sixteen releases to his name, and he has recorded with artists including Joey DeFrancesco, Van Morrison and Kurt Elling.

His latest album is Green Lights, which features John Patitucci, Paul Bollenback and Jimmy Macbride. Tonight's lineup is Roberts on saxophone, Devin Thai on piano, Lois Majors on bass and Austin McMahon on drums.

The evening

The show is presented by the New England Jazz Collaborative with Arrow Street Arts. Doors open at 6:30 PM, and the music runs from 7:30 to 9:30 PM, according to the event page. The Studio is at 2 Arrow Street in Cambridge, Massachusetts, so the concert suits a weeknight outing for anyone who wants a small room and a full quartet sound.
