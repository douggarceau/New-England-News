Last Call for 'Wicked' at the Citizens Opera House: Closes Nov. 15

The Broadway blockbuster's fall run on Washington Street ends in just over two weeks.

If you have been meaning to see Wicked in Boston this fall, the clock is ticking. Broadway In Boston's run of the musical at the Citizens Opera House ends Sunday, Nov. 15, after opening Sept. 23.

The show, which tells the backstory of the witches of Oz before Dorothy ever drops in, remains one of the most reliable crowd-pleasers on the touring circuit, with a big score, big sets and one of the best-known first-act closers in musical theater. It came to town as an add-on to the 2026/27 Broadway In Boston subscription season, so single tickets have been the main way in.

The details

The Citizens Opera House is at 539 Washington St. in Downtown Crossing, a short walk from the Orange and Red lines at Downtown Crossing and the Green Line at Boylston. Tickets are sold through Broadway In Boston. With closing weekend approaching, the best seat selection is usually on weeknights.

What comes next in the Theater District

The Opera House stage turns over quickly. Boston Ballet moves in with The Nutcracker on Nov. 27, and Broadway In Boston's subscription season continues at the Emerson Colonial Theatre with The Notebook, Dec. 8 to 20. In the new year, Death Becomes Her plays the Opera House Jan. 12 to 24, followed by Maybe Happy Ending, Feb. 2 to 14.
