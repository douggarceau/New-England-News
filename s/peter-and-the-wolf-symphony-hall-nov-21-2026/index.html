Peter and the Wolf Gets a Family Concert at Symphony Hall, Nov. 21

Saturday, Nov. 21, at 12:00 PM at Symphony Hall, 301 Massachusetts Avenue, with the Boston Youth Symphony Orchestras and the Boston Ballet School.

The Boston Youth Symphony Orchestras and the Boston Ballet School present Prokofiev's Peter and the Wolf at Symphony Hall on Saturday, Nov. 21, at 12:00 PM. The event is part of the BSO's family concert series.

Federico Cortese is listed as music director of the Boston Youth Symphony Orchestras, and Tamara King as associate director of the Boston Ballet School. The page describes the work as a symphony fairy tale in which each character is given its own tune on a specific instrument.

The story follows adventure, teamwork, bravery and compassion, and the page offers the bird as the flute and the cat as the clarinet as examples of how the instruments are matched to the characters. It is an easy first concert for young listeners.

Tickets

The family concert series package is listed at $60 per adult. Tickets are sold through the Buy Tickets link on the BSO's event page.
