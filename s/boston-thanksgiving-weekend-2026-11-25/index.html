Boston's Long Weekend: Markets, Messiah and a Last Plymouth Call

Snowport and SoWa close Thursday and stay open late Friday, Plimoth Patuxet's season ends Sunday, and Fabolous plays Encore Friday night.

Four days off and a fridge full of leftovers. Here's what's actually open around Boston once the plates are cleared.

Thursday: mostly closed

The Museum of Fine Arts and the ICA are both closed on Thanksgiving, and so are the big holiday markets, according to Encore Boston Harbor's weekend guide. Plan the outing for Friday.

The markets

The SoWa Christmas Market in the South End has more than 100 makers and keeps Black Friday hours of 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. The Holiday Market at Snowport in the Seaport has more than 130 makers and runs 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Friday. Both stay open through Dec. 27, per the same guide.

Culture

The MFA's Egyptian show, Maverick Kings: Three Visionary Pharaohs of Egypt's Golden Age, closes Dec. 6 and needs a timed ticket, so this weekend is one of its last good shots. Handel and Haydn Society's Messiah at Symphony Hall plays Friday at 7:30 p.m. and Saturday and Sunday at 3 p.m. Down in Plymouth, the Plimoth Patuxet Museums' Historic Patuxet Homesite and 17th-century English Village wrap their season on Sunday, Nov. 29.

Late nights

At Encore Boston Harbor's Mémoire club, Fabolous plays Friday and Costa takes Saturday, both from 10 p.m., 21 and over.
