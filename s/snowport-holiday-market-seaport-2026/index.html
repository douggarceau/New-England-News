Snowport Returns to the Seaport With 130 Makers and Live Music

Opens Friday, Nov. 6, at 100 Seaport Boulevard in the Seaport District, with weekend hours from 10 a.m. and the market running through Dec. 27.

Snowport, the holiday market that has become a fixture of Boston's Seaport District, opened its sixth season on Friday, Nov. 6, at 100 Seaport Boulevard. More than 130 independent businesses are taking part, including 17 local food and beverage vendors, and Boston 25 News reports that most of them are based in New England.

Shoppers will find apparel, jewelry, home decor, artwork, toys, gourmet gifts and other items from local makers. Boston.com's listing adds a Christmas tree market, iceless curling that can be reserved through Feb. 28, 2027, and photo spots. Live music is back this season: Mohegan Music Mondays features emerging Boston-area performers from Berklee College of Music starting at 5 p.m. each Monday, and more music is planned from Tuesday through Thursday, according to Boston 25.

Hours and entry

General admission is no charge. From Nov. 6 through Dec. 6 the market is open Monday through Thursday from 3 to 8 p.m., Friday from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., Saturday from 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Weekday hours start at 11 a.m. from Dec. 7. The market is closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day and closes at 6 p.m. on Christmas Eve. Weekend timed-entry passes are reserved through the Seaport Insider app, at $15 for adults, according to Boston 25.

Beyond the stalls

The market also includes a Creativity Corner for craft projects such as snow globe making, as WBUR's weekend preview describes it. The holiday calendar continues on Dec. 4 at 6 p.m., when a tree lighting takes place at 85 Northern Avenue, and a menorah lighting follows on Dec. 10 at 6 p.m. at 60 Seaport Boulevard, according to Boston.com.

Snowport is a reminder that the holiday season in Boston does not have to be spent in one crowded hall. For an afternoon it offers a gift from a local studio, a student's set on a Monday evening, and a chance to make something by hand before the lights go up across the city. The market runs through Dec. 27, so there is time to return.
