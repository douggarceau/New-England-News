Pelin Su Yavuz Plays Piano at the Shaw-Roxbury Library, Nov. 14

Saturday, Nov. 14 at noon at the Shaw-Roxbury Branch Library, 149 Dudley Street in Roxbury, part of the Roxbury Concert Series.

On a Saturday at midday, a branch library in Roxbury becomes a concert hall. Pelin Su Yavuz, a pianist whose path runs from Istanbul to Symphony Hall, will perform at the Shaw-Roxbury Branch of the Boston Public Library on Nov. 14, as part of the Roxbury Concert Series.

The concert

The recital begins at 12 p.m. at the branch, 149 Dudley Street. The Roxbury Concert Series presents it in association with Vivo Performing Arts. Admission is no charge, and the series says its events are open to all. Guests are asked to reserve a seat through RoxburyConcertSeries.org. The event page does not list the program, so come ready to hear what the pianist chooses.

About the pianist

The presenters describe Yavuz as a Boston Calling headliner and a Boston Pops performer, and they say she is reimagining what a young pianist can do. A Saturday noon recital is a relaxed way to hear her live.

Why it is worth the trip

The Roxbury Concert Series is part of Vivo Performing Arts' effort to bring serious music into the neighborhood's libraries and community spaces. The organizers thank Lizbeth and George Krupp for leading Vivo's programming in Roxbury. For neighbors, families and anyone who has not been inside the Shaw-Roxbury branch, it is an easy and unhurried way to spend a Saturday.

Getting there

The branch is on Dudley Street in Dudley Square, close to Dudley Station on the Orange Line. Bring a friend, and plan to arrive a few minutes early to find a seat.
