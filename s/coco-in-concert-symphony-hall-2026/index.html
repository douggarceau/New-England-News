Coco in Concert: Boston Pops Scores Pixar's Film Live at Symphony Hall

Saturday, Oct. 31 at 1:30 p.m. at Symphony Hall, 301 Massachusetts Ave., Back Bay.

The Boston Pops, led by Keith Lockhart, is performing the score of Disney and Pixar's Coco live while the film plays above the stage at Symphony Hall. The matinee on Saturday, October 31, begins at 1:30 p.m., making it a bright, family-friendly afternoon in Back Bay.

The story follows Miguel, a boy whose family has banned music, who dreams of becoming a musician like his idol, Ernesto de la Cruz. On the Day of the Dead he slips into the Land of the Dead, meets the trickster Héctor, and sets out to uncover the truth about his family's history. Hearing the orchestra play the film's songs in the hall, keeping pace with the picture, is a rich way to experience the movie.

Plan Your Visit

Symphony Hall is at 301 Massachusetts Avenue, a short walk from Symphony station on the Green Line. Tickets are sold through the Boston Symphony Orchestra, and its listing notes that tickets start at $58. Subscription packages with discounts are also available through the orchestra's Choose Your Own Series, which requires at least three performances.

Tickets for the Saturday matinee are at https://secure.bso.org/syos/performance/28654
