Arsenal Holiday Bazaar Brings 50-Plus Small Businesses to Watertown

Saturday, Nov. 14, and Sunday, Nov. 15, from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM at Arsenal Yards, Bond Square in Watertown, with outdoor shopping and food.

The Arsenal Holiday Bazaar takes over Arsenal Yards in Watertown this weekend, running Saturday, Nov. 14, and Sunday, Nov. 15, from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The event is outdoors and rain or shine, and the organizers note that severe weather may reduce hours or days.

Organizers describe more than 50 small local businesses taking part, with a full vendor list still listed as to be announced. Vinyl DJs will spin records during the bazaar, and the site lists more than 20 restaurants and a dozen stores on the grounds.

Getting there and taking part

Arsenal Yards is at Bond Square in Watertown. The event page says parking is at no charge. Makers who want to sell at the bazaar can apply through the link on the event page.
