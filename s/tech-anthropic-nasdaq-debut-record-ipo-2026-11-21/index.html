Anthropic Lands on Nasdaq With the Largest IPO on Record

The Claude maker priced shares at $83, raised about $81 billion and opened its first trading day near $111.

Anthropic, the artificial intelligence lab behind the Claude chatbot and coding tools, became a public company this week. The company priced its initial public offering at $83 a share on Wednesday, Nov. 18, above its projected range of $70 to $78, TechCrunch reported. Shares began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday, Nov. 19.

What happened

According to TechCrunch, Anthropic sold 980 million shares, up from the 825 million it first planned to offer, for about $81.3 billion in proceeds. That figure tops the roughly $75 billion SpaceX raised, making it the largest IPO by proceeds, TechCrunch said. TechRepublic cited the Associated Press describing it as the largest public stock offering on record.

The stock opened at $111.50, about 34 percent above the offering price, TechRepublic reported. Trading cooled during the day: citing live coverage from Business Insider, TechRepublic put shares near $94.60 around 1:20 p.m. Eastern.

How the deal works

Most of the shares in the offering came from the company itself. TechRepublic reported that Anthropic sold about 956.4 million shares, while existing holders sold about 23.6 million, a portion from which the company receives nothing.

Alongside the public sale, Anthropic arranged a separate $26 billion private placement at the same $83 price, in a different share class, with seven buyers including Google, Nvidia, AMD and Microsoft, according to TechRepublic. TechCrunch listed Broadcom, Micron and TSMC Partners among the participants as well. TechRepublic estimated combined net proceeds to the company at about $104.8 billion, with closing expected Nov. 20.

Who owns it

TechCrunch reported that Amazon holds nearly 15 percent of the company and Google almost 14 percent. Each of Anthropic's seven co-founders sold about 3.4 million shares, worth roughly $282 million apiece, based on the company's S-1 filing, TechCrunch said.

The IPO values Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, according to TechCrunch, up from $965 billion in a $65 billion private funding round in May.

Why it matters

The listing gives public investors their first direct stake in one of the handful of labs building the largest AI models. It also puts hard numbers in front of the market. TechRepublic reported revenue of $17 billion for the three months ended Sept. 30, compared with $1.4 billion a year earlier, and noted that nearly all of that revenue comes from a small set of products, mainly Claude.

What comes next

As a public company, Anthropic will now report results each quarter, giving outsiders a regular look at how fast its business is growing and how much it spends on computing power. Neither report gave a date for its first earnings release.
