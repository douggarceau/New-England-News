Gavin Adcock Brings Two Country Nights to MGM Music Hall, Nov. 5-6

The rising country singer plays back-to-back shows at Fenway's concert hall with Braxton Keith and The Creekers.

Country fans get a double shot next week when Gavin Adcock plays MGM Music Hall at Fenway on Thursday, Nov. 5, and Friday, Nov. 6.

Adcock has quickly moved up to rooms the size of MGM Music Hall, one of the city's largest indoor concert halls. Two nights in Boston is a sign of how fast that audience is growing in New England.

Who else is on the bill

Braxton Keith and The Creekers round out the Friday lineup, so come early and catch the whole bill.

The details

Doors open at 7 p.m. MGM Music Hall is at 2 Lansdowne St., tucked against Fenway Park, a short walk from Kenmore on the Green Line. Tickets are on sale through Ticketmaster and SeatGeek; prices were not posted on the listing we checked.

Make a night of it

Lansdowne Street is one of the city's busiest nightlife blocks, with House of Blues next door and plenty of places to grab dinner before the show. On a weeknight, the Green Line to Kenmore beats hunting for parking around the ballpark.
