Quincy Market Tree Lighting on Nov. 21 Brings Santa to Faneuil Hall

Saturday, Nov. 21, from 2:00 to 6:00 PM at Quincy Market in Faneuil Hall, with the tree lighting at 6:00 PM.

Downtown's holiday season starts at Quincy Market on Saturday, Nov. 21, when Faneuil Hall Marketplace holds its annual tree lighting. The celebration runs from 2:00 to 6:00 PM, and the tree lights at 6:00 PM.

The listing says Make-A-Wish and Santa will take part, which makes the afternoon an easy outing for families. Admission is at no charge, and the listing does not mention any registration, so you can simply arrive.

A little history

The market building dates to 1826 and takes its name from Josiah Quincy, the Boston mayor who oversaw its construction. Today the hall is a busy downtown landmark, and its food and gift stalls make it an easy place to spend an afternoon.

Bring the family

The afternoon suits a relaxed visit with children. The tree gives the day a clear focal point, and the 6:00 PM lighting brings the event to its close, so you can time a walk through the market to end at the tree.

Before you go

The listing does not give a street address, so Quincy Market in the Faneuil Hall area of downtown Boston is the place to head. Plan to arrive a little before 6:00 PM if you want to see the lighting itself. Faneuil Hall Marketplace organizes the event, and its listing is the best place to check for any change to the schedule before you go.
