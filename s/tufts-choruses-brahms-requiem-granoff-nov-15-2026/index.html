Tufts Choirs Bring Brahms's German Requiem to Medford, Nov. 15

Sunday, Nov. 15 at 3:00 PM at the Granoff Music Center's Distler Hall in Medford: the Tufts Concert Choir and Chamber Singers, with no charge admission.

The Tufts University Concert Choir and Chamber Singers present a Sunday afternoon of choral music on November 15, 2026, at 3:00 PM in the Distler Performance Hall of the Granoff Music Center, 20 Talbot Avenue in Medford. The concert is conducted by Jamie Kirsch and accompanied by pianists Thomas Stumpf and Edith Auner, and the listing gives an end time of 4:30 PM.

What you will hear

The program includes selections from Brahms's A German Requiem, a large-scale work for chorus that Brahms wrote for the consolation of the living, alongside music by Jenkins, Esmail, Wright and Dello Joio.

Brahms wrote the work in German rather than Latin, and the Tufts program offers a chance to hear its choral writing in a university setting.

Admission and planning

Brahms completed A German Requiem over several years, and the full work was first performed in Bremen in 1868. Its texts are drawn from the Lutheran Bible rather than the Latin Mass, which sets it apart from the requiem settings most listeners know.

Admission is no charge. The listing does not say whether reservations are needed, so check the Tufts event page before heading out. The concert is a good afternoon for anyone who wants to hear a university choir and its chamber singers in a hall built for music.
