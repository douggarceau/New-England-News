Victoria Monét Brings Frequency of Love to MGM Music Hall, Nov. 13

Friday, Nov. 13, at 8:00 PM at MGM Music Hall at Fenway, 2 Lansdowne Street in Fenway, on the Frequency of Love Tour.

Grammy-winning singer Victoria Monét brings her Frequency of Love Tour to Boston on Friday, Nov. 13, with a show that starts at 8:00 PM at MGM Music Hall at Fenway. The tour supports her new album and was announced in September with dates across North America this fall.

The venue sits at 2 Lansdowne Street, beside Fenway Park. The calendar listing for the show gives an 8:00 PM start and runs until 11:00 PM. It does not list an entry time, so check the ticket page for that detail before you head out.

Getting tickets

Tickets are sold through the event listing linked from our calendar. The listing we checked did not state a price, so look for the current prices when you buy.
