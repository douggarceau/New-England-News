This Weekend in Boston: The Creation at Symphony Hall, Patriots-Packers and a Rare Book Fair

The BSO sings Haydn, Boston Lyric Opera stages A Streetcar Named Desire, the Antiquarian Book Fair fills the Hynes, and the Bruins and Patriots play Sunday, Nov. 6 to 8.

Boston's first full weekend of November has opera downtown, Haydn at Symphony Hall, a 48th-year book fair in the Back Bay and a full Sunday of pro sports. Here are the weekend's top events, Friday through Sunday.

Boston International Antiquarian Book Fair

The 48th annual Boston International Antiquarian Book Fair opens Friday at 4 p.m. at the Hynes Convention Center. A $25 Friday ticket is good for the whole fair.

Comedy

Tim Dillon plays the Wilbur Theatre Friday at 7:30 and 10 p.m. and Saturday at 9:45 p.m. Drew Dunn is at the Wilbur Saturday at 7 p.m., and DeRay Davis on Sunday at 7 p.m.

Out and about

Arlington Open Studios, in its 28th year, opens Saturday at 11 a.m., with Arlington Town Hall as the hub; there's no admission charge. On Friday at 7:30 p.m., the Berklee Performance Center screens the Warren Miller ski film Days Off.
