About the Publisher

These sites are published by Island News Media Productions LLC, a Rhode Island company founded by Doug Garceau, its Founder, Editor and Director. Doug is a former rock music journalist from Seattle, where he wrote the column Raising Hell with Guitar Doug, and a formally trained drummer and percussionist. Island News Media publishes Jamestown Island News, Rhode Island Hurricane, New York City Scream and The Boston Rambler, and The Names He Carried, a nonfiction family history with its Academic Edition.
